Duhaa — Privacy Policy

Effective June 9, 2026

The short version: Duhaa has no accounts, ads, analytics, or trackers. Your worship data stays on your device. Duhaa uses a few Apple and Quran.com services only when you ask for features that need them.

What stays on your device

Your prayer marks, streaks and insights, fasting logs, tasbih counts, Quran bookmarks, notes, downloads, saved mosques, tab layout, and settings are stored in the app's local storage on your phone. We have no Duhaa server and no way to see any of it. Deleting the app deletes this data unless you made your own export or backup.

Location

Prayer times and Qibla direction require a location. Prayer-time and Qibla calculations happen on your device. If you use current location, city search, reverse geocoding, or Nearby Mosques, Apple location and MapKit services process the request. If you prefer, you can skip location access and pick a city by hand from the offline city list.

The only network connections

Duhaa makes no network calls except these:

  • Quran.com / Quran Foundation — recitation audio, chapter timing, word-by-word timing, and some Mushaf page data are requested when you use those Quran features. As with any web server, that service sees the network request, including your IP address. Duhaa does not attach prayer data or tracking identifiers.
  • Apple location and MapKit — used for current location, city naming, city search, Apple Maps directions, and Nearby Mosques.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — used only for voluntary Duhaa+ support purchases, restore, manage subscription, and offer-code flows.
  • Your email app — if you send feedback, report a bug, request a reciter, or suggest a mosque, your mail app opens a draft to Duhaa. You choose what to send.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No push servers are involved and nothing about your prayers is transmitted.

Children

Duhaa collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Since the app's design is local-only, we don't expect it to change much.
